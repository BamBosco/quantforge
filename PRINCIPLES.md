# QuantForge Principles

1. Capital preservation above everything.
2. Probability over prediction.
3. Evidence over opinion.
4. Every edge must be measurable.
5. Simplicity beats complexity when outcomes are equivalent.
6. Modules communicate through explicit contracts.
7. Risk precedes return.
8. Automation removes predictable human error.
9. Every material decision is explainable and auditable.
10. The same inputs must produce the same outputs.
11. Validation is continuous: historical, walk-forward, Monte Carlo, and forward testing.
12. Optimize for long-term durability.
13. Every trade is data for improvement.
14. Research and production are separate.
15. Challenge every assumption.
16. Code is readable, modular, tested, documented, and maintainable.
17. Data provenance and quality determine what can be trusted.
18. Sources are hypotheses, never doctrine.
19. The platform must earn trust through consistent operation.
20. QuantForge is a professional system, not a trading bot.
