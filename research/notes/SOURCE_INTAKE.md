# Source Intake Register

This register separates supplied source material from QuantForge policy and
records whether it is suitable for strategy research. A source may describe an
idea without providing evidence that the idea is profitable.

## Required high-priority additions

- The relevant complete Thomas Bulkowski ORB chapter or a precise bibliographic
  citation that permits review.
- ORB academic papers or reproducible studies, including dataset, market,
  sample period, cost assumptions, and methodology.

## Intake rules

For every source, record author, title, edition/publication date, source path
or link, rights/usage status, claims being investigated, and limitations. Do
not copy source rules into a strategy specification until they are explicitly
chosen by the project owner and testable without ambiguity.
